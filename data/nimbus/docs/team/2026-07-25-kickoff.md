# Kickoff — Nimbus 2027 app

## What we're building

A web app for Nimbus 2027: registrations, payments, schedule, tickets and check-in.
Aarav leads tech. Priya runs the board and the weekly sync (Saturdays, 6 pm).

## Decisions

- **PWA only.** No native Android app this year. Nobody has time to maintain two apps, and
  a PWA installs fine on Android.
- **Payments: Razorpay.** It supports UPI, the fees are low for small tickets, and KYC can go
  through the club's account, which Priya already manages.
- **Database: Supabase Postgres.** The treasurer needs SQL exports for the college audit.
  Firestore was fine for the prototype but exporting from it was painful.
